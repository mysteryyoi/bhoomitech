# Farm site reference audit and build decisions

27 November 2026. This records the reference mechanics to rebuild in Bhoomi Tech's Astro project. The homepage uses Diesel Farm's approach to telling a farm story. The produce catalogue uses Agrumea Farm's approach to browsing and comparing products. The implementation starts with Bhoomi Tech's routes, copy, photos, illustrations and visual system.

## Diesel Farm

| Area | What the reference does | How it translates to Bhoomi Tech |
|---|---|---|
| Home and menu | The homepage links visual chapters for Farm, Ecosystem, Cellar, Products, Kitchen and Contacts, plus Experiences. A collage is visible behind the age prompt. The studio describes composing the artwork from photographs taken on the estate and illustrations. | Open on a real wide view of the polyhouse. Build five original spreads around one lettuce: seed, inside the polyhouse, harvest, seller, plate. Keep direct routes to produce, farm software and farmer services visible from the opening screen. |
| Farm and Ecosystem | The farm story alternates landscape and people with explanations of place and cultivation. The ecosystem page connects photographs of the terrain and crops with specific practices. | Use the farm entrance, an interior aisle, crop rows and hands at work as distinct views. Brief captions identify the actual activity, equipment or growing detail. A soil-to-daylight palette and lime annotations tie the spreads together. |
| Cellar, Products and Kitchen | The cellar explains a production process, then presents products. The kitchen connects ingredients from the estate with food on a plate. The main products route timed out during review. The homepage links to it and the public store confirms the outbound shopping path. | Carry the same crop through the grow, harvest and table moments. Use verified farm photos as the bridge into Our Produce. Bhoomi Tech's purchase action leads to its own authorized seller listings. |
| Experiences and Contacts | The experience page gives practical details beside contact actions. Contact provides distinct WhatsApp destinations and a form. | Retain Bhoomi Tech's separate software-demo and farmer-enquiry routes. Use the direct-contact pattern for the relevant activity. Publish visits or tours only if Bhoomi Tech actually offers them. |

**Verification limit.** The browser showed an age attestation before the homepage interaction could be used. I verified the menu structure and public page content and saw the composition behind the prompt. Exact behavior after the prompt remains unverified, including whether the site pins spreads, maps wheel input to a timeline, or treats hover and touch differently. The behavior below is a build proposal, so it does not depend on knowing Diesel Farm's implementation.

## Agrumea Farm

| Area | What the reference does | How it translates to Bhoomi Tech |
|---|---|---|
| Home and menu | Scrolling brings photographed product models into view. A visible product action links to an individual detail. The menu provides direct links to Pantry, Production, Experience, About and Contacts and an external Shop link. | Add an optional crop turntable or scan inside the harvest spread. Keep the crop name and its ordinary link discoverable on touch and keyboard. The main menu continues to use Bhoomi Tech's own routes. |
| Pantry | An editorial introduction leads to grouped cards. The compact category rail remains in view while browsing. Clicking Creams scrolled to its section on the same page. Clicking Almond Spread opened its own URL. | Build a category rail for Lettuce, Kale and Cucumber. A category behaves as an anchor. Show a large crop photo plus a short description, then any verified varieties or pack options. Each card opens a static detail URL. Put a crop-specific seller action on the catalogue and the detail page. |
| Product detail | A prominent product photo sits with origin, usage tips, pack options and storage or shelf information, followed by more products. | Use the same photo family as the index, with an approved growing description, variety, handling or pack information where available. Include links to the other two crops and the exact seller list. Keep seller authorization, crop listing and current stock separate. |
| Production | The separate production page lists fresh varieties, harvest windows and case or pack information. | This is the better reference for fresh crop facts. Show only confirmed seasonal windows and formats. With three crops, simple rows suffice. An editorial field can be omitted until the farm supplies it. |
| Experience, About and Contacts | Experience describes what visitors can do. About explains the place and its people. Contact presents the business's accessible routes. | Apply the same direct link from story to relevant action. Describe only services and visits Bhoomi Tech can really offer. |

## Implementation in Bhoomi Tech

1. **Prototype one scrapbook spread: entering the polyhouse.** Use a wide frame as the visual anchor. As the next chapter approaches, an interior aisle photo settles forward, one close-up enters beside it and a caption identifies what the visitor is seeing. A fixed-aspect figure prevents surrounding content from jumping. Use layered HTML and CSS with transforms. If the editorial timing calls for scrubbing, use a small GSAP ScrollTrigger module for that spread. On touch, low-capability devices and reduced motion, show finished spreads with captions in reading order. Keyboard navigation and chapter links remain native. [ScrollTrigger documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/).
2. **Prototype one complete catalogue path: Lettuce.** The route is `/produce/` → lettuce anchor → `/produce/lettuce/` → the lettuce seller page. Start with ordinary links, stable IDs and a clear return path. Use the existing producer and seller data plan to generate static routes. A small optional scroll observer can highlight the active crop. Category links, crop pages and seller links work with scripts turned off. [Astro routing documentation](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/routing/).
3. **Add the other crops and chapters using the same templates.** Kale and cucumber get equal visible catalogue entries. Use a shot list for different scales: polyhouse exterior, entrance, interior aisle, row, hands and harvest, then a consistent portrait for each crop. A real scan or short turntable sequence can be layered in as the enhanceable harvest moment. Check the existing device tiers and performance budgets before loading it on the first screen.

Review the first spread on desktop and a small phone, then follow the catalogue path with a mouse, keyboard and JavaScript disabled. The first build milestone is both journeys working with original material. The full art direction depends on having real farm and crop photos cleared for the site.
