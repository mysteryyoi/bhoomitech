# Bhoomi Tech · Brand, Home and About

Independent creative planning by C1, OpenAI gpt-6.2-astra. Based on the owner’s shared brief and Codex assignment dated 26 November 2026. Suggested headlines below are editorial drafts.

## 1. Purpose and creative direction

**Put one head of lettuce at the centre of the site.** A seed held between two fingers, a growing leaf, a harvested head, produce offered by a seller, and a leaf on a plate give “seed to fork” a subject a visitor can follow. Present the sequence as a conceptual journey and reserve claims about a tracked batch or an actual farm-to-retailer route for independently verified records. Frame the subject at the same point in each composition. The cut from one scale to the next supplies the sense of movement. Ordinary vegetables should look worth a second look.

Home gives each visitor a useful exit within the first screen. A household can find a seller, a software buyer can explore farm automation, and a farmer can reach a person about services. Visitors who stay encounter one continuous illustrated story showing how those needs fit together. About answers a different question. Which parts of this journey does Bhoomi Tech actually do, who does the work and how can I ask them about it?

**Identity and art direction**

- Keep the geometric B and the `bhoomitech.` wordmark, including the coloured full stop. Use “Bhoomi Tech” in sentences and accessible names. Give the existing stacked hexagons one job. Adapt their profile into a shallow, isometric display tray under the photographic subject. A single plane comes forward during the story, giving the old sculpture a recognisable role. Keep it clearly illustrative.
- Use dark green `#101610` for the opening mat around the produce and for body text on light surfaces. Add a warm, uncoated-paper ground, proposed `#F3EFE4`, for the story text and About. Reserve lime `#b8ed79` for the active chapter, keyboard focus on dark backgrounds and one main action per region. Pair lime buttons with dark text. Use deep green `#294a20` links and focus outlines on paper. Use `#629e36` for broad graphic shapes, and `#e2ffb0` for fine marks on dark. Test final combinations on their actual backgrounds. Every selected state also gets a shape, underline or written label.
- Set display lines in **Bricolage Grotesque**, with **Source Sans 3** for paragraphs, links, captions and forms. The display face has enough character for a two-line headline without turning technical. - Keep copy short enough to say aloud. Prefer “Find a seller”, “Explore farm software” and “Ask about your farm”. Each link names its destination. ## 2. Page sequence and copy

### Home

1. **Masthead and opening still life.** Lead with “From seed to fork.” Follow with “Bhoomi Tech grows and supplies vegetables and develops AI solutions for farms.” Give “Find a seller” the main button and show “Explore farm software” and “Services for farmers” as prominent text links. Put the portrait of the lettuce opposite the text. The headline, all three links and an identifiable piece of the image should fit into a typical first phone screen. An ordinary “Follow the lettuce” anchor introduces the longer story below.
2. **Seed.** Begin on paper with the seed at actual hand scale, then let the art show an enlarged detail. Draft line “Small enough to hold between two fingers.” In one sentence introduce lettuce as one of the vegetables Bhoomi Tech grows. Identify any specimen illustration by common name. Avoid specifying a cultivar or making claims about seed supply until confirmed.
3. **AI-run farm.** A leaf enters the same position previously occupied by the seed. “Where software meets the growing season.” Explain that Bhoomi Tech develops software for automating farm operations. An adjacent link opens Farm Software, where the separate product demonstration belongs. Showing software as part of the company's offering requires no claim that this lettuce grew in an instrumented farm. Use [PLACEHOLDER: confirmed application on Bhoomi Tech’s own farm] before connecting a real crop photograph to an automation claim.
4. **Harvest.** Let the head fill the frame, including its imperfect outer leaves. “What we grow.” Surface lettuce, kale and cucumbers from the produce list as links. Use real produce-page destinations and the spelling in the shared content file. This is the first clean, full-product view and should remain legible in a still screenshot.
5. **Authorized sellers.** Pull back to seller-counter scale. “Where to find it.” Say that households buy through authorized shops and marketplaces and link directly to Where to Buy. Replace the conceptual counter illustration with an identified retailer photograph only when [PLACEHOLDER: seller approval and image rights] is supplied. The story illustrates the route to a seller. It carries no promise of stock today.
6. **Plate and onward routes.** A single leaf occupies the same part of a cream plate that the lettuce occupied on the counter. “And onto your plate.” Put the seller link beside that last frame. Below it, repeat the two farm routes with short descriptions of who each serves. Use “Meet Bhoomi Tech” as a quiet text link above the footer. Leave the visual conclusion intact, without a carousel or a new gallery competing with it.

### About

1. **Introduce the company in plain language.** Draft heading “The people and work behind Bhoomi Tech.” Open with two factual lines about growing and supplying vegetables, farm automation and services for farmers. Beside them place [PLACEHOLDER: verified farm or team image, caption, credit and permission]. Before such an image exists, lead with a closely cropped commissioned lettuce drawing from Home and title the page “About Bhoomi Tech”.
2. **Explain the work and its handoffs.** Use four short entries in running text, headed “Seed-to-fork services”, “Marketplace services”, “AI solutions” and “Modern farming solutions”. For each, name who it serves, the activity the company performs and its next destination. Request [PLACEHOLDER: one approved practical example and boundary per offering]. For marketplace services specifically, explain the authorized-seller route households can use. Obtain confirmation before describing ownership of a marketplace, packing facilities, transport or fulfilment.
3. **Show the farm and people where evidence exists.** Build a restrained photo-and-caption spread. A single authentic image accompanied by “what is happening, who is involved, where this was taken” can explain a great deal. Use [PLACEHOLDER: approved names, roles, location and first-hand account]. If only images can be cleared, run a captioned farm spread. If neither images nor details can be cleared, omit the section at launch. Permit a short first-person account later without reserving empty portrait slots now.
4. **Close with the right person or place.** “What brings you here?” Offer Where to Buy, Farm Software, and For Farmers with one line beside each. Include Contact for general correspondence. Keep links visible when text wraps in a longer language.

## 4. Signature moments and motion language

**The lettuce match cut, Home.** In a wide viewport the right-hand frame sticks only within the five chapters. When the next heading crosses the middle third of the viewport, stage its composition at the same registration point. The geometric tray lifts one shallow plane, the old image yields to the new over roughly 240 ms, and the plane settles. Reverse scrolling selects the earlier frame. Quick chapter jumps go directly to the destination image. The page uses native scroll and the last chapter releases the frame. For reduced motion, use inline stills, no sticky panel, no lift or dissolve. Low-end Android and constrained connections get the same inline sequence using small, locally stored illustrations, loaded as each approaches view. Render the first still with the initial page. A visitor can read and follow every link before enhancement loads or if it fails.

**A leaf at two scales, Home opening.** Supply a visible “See leaf detail” button beside the portrait. On activation, a bounded inset opens within the image mat and shows a second, optically sharp crop of the veins at its centre. Keep the leaf stationary so both views can be compared. The label changes to “Close detail”. Keyboard activation and touch perform the same action. Use a brief opacity change on capable devices. For reduced motion, switch instantly. On low-end Android, use a small pre-cropped still loaded on request beneath the image, with its own textual caption. If good macro material is unavailable, omit the control. About relies on photography, spacing and captions and needs no third effect.

Sitewide, focus cues appear immediately, hover adds an underline and chapter state has a printed name. Avoid scroll capture and background video. Optional depth rendering should approximate the approved static tray closely enough that the fallback remains equally art-directed. Commissioning better source images takes priority over implementing the tray in 3D.
