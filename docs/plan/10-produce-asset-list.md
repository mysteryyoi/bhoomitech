## The rule

The packs, the vegetables and the food made from them are all Bhoomi Tech's own, so **every picture on these pages is a real photo**. ChatGPT makes nothing here.

Until the photos exist, the hidden preview shows a "Photo to come" card where each pack goes. It can borrow the collage's generated vegetables as stand-ins. None of it goes live.

## 1. Real photos (a phone is fine)

### One setup for every product photo

So that all the packs match, shoot them all the same way, ideally on the same day.

- **Light:**
  - indoors beside a big window, in daylight but not direct sun
  - or outdoors in open shade on an overcast day
  - no flash
- **Background:** a plain white or very light grey sheet (chart paper or a bedsheet). Run it from the wall down and under the pack in one curve, so there's no line behind it.
- **Phone:**
  - on a stand, level with the middle of the pack, about 1 m away
  - use the 2× lens if you have one, so the pack doesn't look stretched
  - press and hold on the pack to lock focus and brightness
- **Keep it the same:** the same spot, distance and light for every pack, so they come out the same size and colour.
- **The packs:** fresh produce inside, with no dents, creases, price stickers or fingerprints. Shoot two of each and keep the better one.
- **Sending:**
  - full-size originals, with no filters and no editing
  - **not through WhatsApp**, which shrinks them
  - use Google Drive, a USB stick or email instead

### P1. Pack shots: the most important

Take these for **every pack: each crop, in each size.**

| Shot | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Front, straight on | The card and the top of the product page |
| Back, straight on | The product page, and to read what's printed |
| Turned about 30° to the left | Makes the tilt look real |
| Turned about 30° to the right | Same |
| Close-up of the label | The photo strip |

**Depending on the pack's shape:**
- **A round tub or cup:**
  - Also do a turntable: the pack on a lazy Susan, one photo every 10°, so 36 photos.
  - Then the pack can turn as you scroll, like Agrumea's jar.
- **A flat pouch or bag:** also one shot of it lying flat, from straight above.
- **A box or tray:** also one shot from a little above, so you see the top and the front.

### P2. The opening photo

All the packs together on a real kitchen table or shop counter, with hands reaching for them.
- **Framing:** landscape and wide, with empty space at the top for the page title.
- **People:** if a face shows, get a signed release.

### P3. One "in use" photo per crop

Real food made with the vegetable, **with its pack in the shot**. Some ideas:
- **Lettuce:** a salad bowl, or a sandwich being made
- **Kale:** a dish your customers would really cook with it
- **Cucumber:** slices on a plate, raita, or a lunchbox

Landscape, natural light, warm. Hands at work are welcome. That's 3 photos.

### P4. The tumbling pieces

These fall in behind the pack when someone points at a card, and they replace Agrumea's flat drawn fruit.

- **How to shoot:**
  - from straight above, on the white sheet
  - each piece on its own, with a hand's width of space around it
  - fresh and dry, with no water drops
- **What to shoot:**

  | Crop | Pieces |
  |---|---|
  | Lettuce | 8 single leaves, of different sizes, a few of them torn |
  | Kale | 8 leaves or curly leaf tips |
  | Cucumber | 8 round slices, 2 halves cut lengthways, 1 small whole cucumber |

  That's about 27 pieces, an afternoon's work in a kitchen.

### P5. Photo strip for each vegetable's page (3 to 5 per crop)

- The pack in a fridge or on a kitchen counter
- The pack on a real shop's shelf, with the shop owner's written permission
- A close-up of the leaves, or the cucumber's skin
- The crop growing on your farm (this also links the page to the collage)
- A hand holding the pack

Landscape, full size.

### P6. The vegetables out of the pack

A whole lettuce head, a bunch of kale and two or three cucumbers, each one alone on the white sheet with the same setup as P1.

These are also what the collage still needs: they replace the generated lettuce, kale and cucumbers in its "What grows" chapter.

## 2. Facts for each pack (text, not photos)

Your own words are fine. Claude tidies the wording, and you approve it before anything goes live.

| What | Notes |
|---|---|
| The name on the pack | Exactly as printed |
| Variety | For example, romaine or iceberg; curly kale |
| Pack sizes | Weight or count, and the pack type: pouch, box, tray or tub |
| Two lines on taste and texture | How you'd describe it to a friend |
| How to store it, and how long it keeps | From your own experience, not a guess |
| Good with | 3 or 4 everyday uses |
| How and where it's grown | Open field, polyhouse, hydroponic and so on, and roughly where |
| Claims on the pack | "Organic", "pesticide-free" and similar only with a certificate. Otherwise they stay off the site. |
| Names in other languages | Only if they're printed on the pack, or you want them shown |
| Where to buy | The shops and online stores that stock each pack, with their OK to be named |
